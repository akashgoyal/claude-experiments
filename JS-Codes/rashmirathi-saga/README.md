# Rashmirathi, Retold (रश्मिरथी · कर्ण की कथा)

A 4-minute-49-second hand-drawn, cut-paper collage film of Karna's whole story, in seven cantos,
retold in new rhymed Hindi verse and inspired by Ramdhari Singh 'Dinkar''s *Rashmirathi* (1952).

**Watch it live:** https://akashgoyal.github.io/claude-experiments/JS-Codes/rashmirathi-saga/

| Chapter | Story |
|---|---|
| Opening | Kunti sets baby Karna adrift; Radha and Adhiratha raise him |
| 1 · रंगभूमि | The archery contest, the insult "सूत-पुत्र", and Duryodhana making him king of Anga |
| 2 · गुरु का शाप | The insect bite at Parashurama's hermitage, and the guru's curse |
| 3 · कृष्ण की चेतावनी | Krishna's peace mission, his cosmic form and warning, and his offer to Karna |
| 4 · दानवीर | Karna gives his armour and earrings to Indra, who gives him a one-use spear |
| 5 · माँ कुंती | Kunti reveals she is his mother; Karna promises to spare four of her sons |
| 6 · अमोघ शक्ति | Karna uses the spear on Ghatotkacha to save the Kaurava army |
| 7 · अंतिम युद्ध | His chariot wheel sinks, the curse takes his mantra, and he falls to Arjuna's arrow |

- Pure JavaScript on a `<canvas>`: no libraries, images, or audio files
- The rhymed verse and chapter titles were written for this film and are **not** Dinkar's text.
  *Rashmirathi* is still under copyright; the film quotes one line from canto 3, credited on screen. Read the full poem.
- Each half-line is recited on a timed grid by the browser's Hindi text-to-speech voice, if the device has one
- Music is synthesized live with the Web Audio API: tanpura, bansuri, sitar, shankh, nagada, bells, and sound effects
- Controls: play/pause (or Space), restart, mute music, voice on/off, a clickable progress bar, and chapter buttons

Open `index.html` in a browser and press **Play with sound**. Chrome and Edge usually include a Hindi voice.

The shorter 89-second film of canto 3 alone is in [`../rashmirathi`](../rashmirathi/).
